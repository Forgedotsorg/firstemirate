## 3. Executive Summary and Company Story

First Emirates Computers and Technical Services L.L.C. is an Abu Dhabi-based IT and security systems company serving since 2011. Its stated primary focus is IT infrastructure and security surveillance systems.

The company positions itself as “Your All in One IT Solution Partner” and states that it aims to provide solutions within the customer’s budget.

The official website describes First Emirates Computers as an IT & Security System Integrator serving the UAE, with products and services covering CCTV surveillance, computers and IT hardware, networking, structured cabling, biometric devices, access control, intercoms, alarms, office infrastructure and technical support.

The web-scraped source describes the company as a cornerstone IT infrastructure and security surveillance systems provider in the UAE and says it has built a robust presence in Abu Dhabi with a mission to deliver comprehensive, technology-driven solutions tailored to diverse business needs.

The web-scraped source says the company positions itself as a single-source provider for organizations seeking integrated IT and security systems without compromise on quality or cost-effectiveness.

The web-scraped source states that the company’s philosophy centers on delivering the best possible solutions within each customer’s budgetary constraints and says it avoids one-size-fits-all approaches by understanding client requirements and sourcing industrial-standard products from trusted, globally recognized brands.

The web-scraped source further describes customer-centric solutions, aggressive pricing, prompt service delivery, and a diverse client base spanning residential, commercial and industrial sectors throughout the UAE.
