## 1. Company Overview

First Emirates Computers and Technical Services L.L.C. (First Emirates Computers) is an Abu Dhabi-based information technology and security systems company serving since 2011. The company operates as an IT solutions provider and IT/security system integrator, with a focus on IT infrastructure, security surveillance, networking, structured cabling, access control and integrated technical solutions. Its stated customer-oriented approach is to provide suitable solutions within customers’ budgets, with customer satisfaction as a priority. The company states that it provides products and services across the United Arab Emirates.

The company’s portfolio brings IT products, security products, installation, system integration, networking, access control, biometric systems, technical support and maintenance together under one provider. Its customer base includes businesses and enterprises as well as residential customers for relevant solutions.
